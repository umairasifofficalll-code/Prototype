// Questions

Good to know before we talk.

Do I need a WhatsApp Business account?

No. Any WhatsApp number works, but WhatsApp Business (free) lets you add quick replies and labels, which helps when orders pick up. We can set it up with you.

Can I change prices or menu items myself?

Yes. We give you a simple way to edit items and prices, and the first month of changes after launch is included if you would rather send them to us.

I only have an Instagram page. Is that enough to start?

That is a great starting point. We reuse your best photos and posts, and your Instagram bio can link straight to the new site.

Do you take a cut of my orders?

No. Customers message you directly, so there is no per-order commission like delivery apps charge.

Are the three demos real businesses?

No — they are concept prototypes with illustrative names, prices and photos, made to show what we can build for your trade.
