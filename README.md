# Lahore business website prototypes

Open `index.html` to browse the three concepts:

- `restaurant.html` — a menu-led charcoal-grill restaurant concept.
- `salon.html` — an appointment-led hair studio concept.
- `garage.html` — an inspection and repair concept.

## Direction notes

- Restaurant: the menu sheet becomes the hero and the menu tabs are the live interaction. Direction seed `733198187`.
- Hair studio: the crossed shears form the signature motif; the main action is to request an appointment. Direction seed `454897833`.
- Garage: wheel-rim geometry repeats through the page; the main action is to request an inspection. Direction seed `124884799`.

These are demonstrations, not real client websites. All business names, addresses, menus, prices, hours, imagery, and email addresses are illustrative. Replace them with each prospect's approved brand details before presenting or publishing. Images were generated for these prototypes and do not depict actual businesses. The forms open email drafts to reserved `example.com` addresses; no booking or enquiry is delivered.
